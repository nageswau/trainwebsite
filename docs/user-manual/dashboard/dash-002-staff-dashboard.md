# Agency dashboard (Staff)

> Doc ID: DOC-DASH-002 · Verified 2026-10-05 against docs commit `717d6aa8` (`main` @ `6a9be770`) · Roles: Agency Staff

## Purpose
See your own work at a glance: your assigned students, their applications and documents, and your next actions.

## Who Can Use This Feature
**Agency Staff.**

## Prerequisites
Students are assigned to you by an agency Master.

## How to Access
Sidebar > **Dashboard** (the page you land on after signing in).

## Steps

### Step 1 — Read your figures
The line "Your assigned students · Your code {code}" confirms the figures count **only your assigned students**.
You see the **Students**, **Pipeline** and **Documents** groups and the country/university breakdowns — but no
**Commission** group and no staff table.

![Staff dashboard](../../screenshots/dashboard/04-staff-dashboard.png)

## Fields
None.

## Expected Result
Figures for your own students; links open your filtered lists.

## Validation Messages
Same as the [Master dashboard](dash-001-master-dashboard.md).

## Common Errors
**Problem:** All figures are 0.
**Cause:** No students are assigned to you yet.
**Resolution:** Ask your agency Master to assign students, or add a student yourself (it is assigned to you).

## Tips
- A **View reports** link appears only if your Master has given you **View reports**.
