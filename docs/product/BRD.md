## 1. Business background / problem

EduSphere UK operates an IT training business — instructor-led courses, real-world projects, career
development, and placement support (EVID-002, EVID-009). Today this runs on manual/fragmented
processes: enquiry handling has no confirmed CRM linkage, trainer/batch assignment and enrolment-ID
visibility are handled ad hoc (EVID-008), live-class recording is constrained by every evaluated
provider's one-session-at-a-time limit, and agent/commission tracking for the business's newer
Overseas Education line is currently manual (EVID-008: "it is getting updated manually only").

The business is now also expanding into **Overseas Education consulting** — helping students apply
to international universities via an agent network, with its own admission, visa, and commission
workflow — a domain not represented at all in the previously-derived planning blueprint
(`docs/evidence/SCOPE_DELTA.md` D-01–D-05).

**Problem statement:** EduSphere needs a single digital platform covering its domestic IT-training
business (public site, student/trainer/admin operations, CRM linkage, employer placement) and its
newer Overseas Education line (destinations, universities, admissions, visa, agent network,
commissions), replacing today's manual coordination.

## 2. Business objectives

1. Provide one platform for the domestic IT-training business: public marketing site, student
   enrolment/learning, trainer delivery, and admin operations, integrated with the confirmed CRM
   (`DEC-SCOPE-001`).
2. Launch the Employer Portal to support the business's placement-services objective
   (`DEC-SCOPE-002`).
3. Launch the Overseas Education line end-to-end: destination/university/course discovery through
   application, visa, and enrolment (`DEC-SCOPE-003`, `DEC-SCOPE-006`).
4. Enable an Agent network with a working commission model to scale Overseas student acquisition
   (`DEC-SCOPE-004`, `DEC-SCOPE-005`).
5. Modernize the technical foundation onto a microservices architecture on DigitalOcean
   (`DEC-ARCH-001`, `DEC-INFRA-001`) — a business objective insofar as it is a confirmed constraint
   on how objectives 1–4 must be delivered; the technical design itself belongs to
   `prompts/08_ARCHITECTURE_AND_ADRS.md`, not this BRD.

**No numeric business target (revenue, enrolment volume, conversion rate, placement rate) exists in
any evidence.** None is invented here — see `BRD_OPEN_ITEMS.md`.

## 7. Business processes / journeys

Business-level only — no UI/screen design (that is `prompts/07`).

1. **Admissions handoff (domestic):** Visitor enquiry → captured as a lead in the **Internal CRM**
   (revised 2026-09-12; was "sync to Zoho CRM" — see `DEC-SCOPE-001`) → Admin routing → student
   creation/import → document verification → approval → enrolment. Steps after lead capture are
   unaffected by the CRM revision.
2. **Domestic student enrolment:** course selection → trainer/time-slot selection (student-selected,
   20/slot cap, locked once booked) → payment (Razorpay, EMI) → access granted → live classes (Zoho)
   → assignments/attendance/certificates.
3. **Overseas applicant journey** (`DEC-WF-001`, base sequence confirmed; exception paths open):
   Enquiry/Interest Capture → Eligibility Evaluation → University/Course Selection → Offer
   ("Unconditional Locker") → Visa Documentation → Status Tracking → Enrolment.
4. **Agent journey:** self-registration → **held pending Overseas Admin approval** → (once approved)
   refer students → view referred students' application status → commission accrues once a referred
   student's application is finalized **and** they join the college → payout requires a **separate**
   Overseas Admin approval step.
5. **Employer journey:** registration → job posting → candidate search (visibility limits open,
   `DEC-PRIV-001`) → interview scheduling.
6. **Trainer/Teacher journey:** conduct live classes (Zoho) → create/manage assignments → validate
   assignments → mark attendance → respond to Q&A.
7. **School journeys** (`DEC-SCOPE-009`/`DEC-SCOPE-011`/`DEC-SCOPE-010`/`DEC-ROLE-005`/
   `DEC-SCOPE-012`, added 2026-09-14, superseded/resolved same day): **Onboarding** — Overseas Admin
   creates the School partner record and a seed School Coordinator account (both active immediately,
   no approval gate) → Coordinator invites/creates Principal, Teacher, and Parent accounts for that
   same institution (also active immediately). **Principal:** logs in → views own-institution
   read-only dashboard/stats/reports. **School
   Coordinator:** logs in → creates/manages student records one at a time, or in bulk via
   download-template → fill offline → upload → validate → row-level accept/reject report →
   schedules activities/tracks attendance/generates reports. **Teacher:** logs in → views assigned
   students' attendance/career activities/progress (read-only). **Parent:** logs in → views own
   child's profile/progress (read-only). Service-delivery data (career guidance, psychometric,
   counselling) is the Counselor's job internally, entirely outside these four journeys, and only
   applies once/if those modules are built (`PENDING`) — no academic results/marks step exists in
   any journey above yet (`DEC-SCOPE-010` part 2, `PENDING`).

## 18. Change-control rules

- No scope change enters this BRD without a corresponding Decision ID resolved in
  `docs/decisions/PRODUCT_DECISION_REGISTER.md` and logged in `docs/product/BRD_CHANGE_LOG.md`.
- A draft test case, a derived document's self-description, or an AI-generated recommendation is
  never sufficient grounds for a scope change — only an explicit user/client confirmation is
  (`CLAUDE.md` prime directive).
- Any change to a `CONFIRMED_CURRENT` decision requires a new dated confirmation, not a silent
  edit — the prior confirmation stays visible in the decision register's history.
