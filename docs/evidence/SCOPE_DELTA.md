## D-08 — Trainer/batch assignment and slot-booking model (domestic scope, not Overseas)

Unlike D-01 through D-07, this item is **not** part of the Overseas/Agent domain — it is additional
detail on the ordinary domestic Student/Trainer relationship that EVID-004/005 already covers only
at a generic level. The blueprint's data-architecture section (§4) lists "trainer assignments" as
part of its Academic domain model, and its Trainer Portal screens (TRN-011 to TRN-018) cover
assignment/submission/attendance management — but neither the blueprint nor EVID-002/EVID-009 says
anything about *how a student gets assigned to a trainer in the first place*, or about time-slot
capacity. EVID-008 does, in detail:

> "students should have a unique ID... That should be created automatically... based on the unique
> ID, [the student] will be assigned into the particular tutor manually or automatically. **I won't
> prefer automatically** in this scenario because... the particular student will always say, I want
> to go to this particular faculty... if there are 2 trainers, **students should have the choice**
> for I want to go for the trainer one['s] sessions, [or] trainer two['s]... We can shift, we can
> make it automatic. The 1st 20 students will be [assigned to the] Python session..."

> "For each batch, we will have separate naming convention... if it is a python [batch], it was
> starting with PY... and it is FSG [for] full stack... while creating the batch, [the admin] will
> create the batch, and who is the trainer for that batch, and who are the students in that batch."

> "...students have the option to select the particular timeline, particular classroom... **this
> lot [slot] is all booked**... **he cannot change his lot**... while signing up, he should select
> this lot... if the class is full... [after] 20 [students]... the slot is completely booked...
> that is fixed for me for the **next 3 months**."

> "we are having the unique ID, but we are not displaying [it] in the UI... [client asks] why we are
> not displaying it in the UI? ... You[r team] are kindly [agreeing] to make the change as well."

Framed as definite, specific business rules (not future-flagged): a preference for **manual,
student-selected** trainer assignment over automatic assignment; a stated **capacity of 20 students
per time-slot**; a **no-change-after-booking** rule lasting the length of the course (~3 months);
a batch-naming convention tied to course/track (e.g. "PY" for Python); and an explicit request to
make the student's unique/enrolment ID visible in the UI (the demoed prototype had it but hid it).

None of this rule set exists in EVID-004/005, EVID-002, or EVID-009 at any level of detail — the
blueprint's "trainer assignments" data-model mention does not specify assignment method, capacity,
or lock-in behavior. This is a gap in the original scope-delta pass and is added here on review.
