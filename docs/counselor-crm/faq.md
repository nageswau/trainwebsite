# Counselor CRM — Frequently asked questions

## Accounts
**Where do I sign in?**
At the **Overseas Education Portal** (`/overseas/login`), not the Administration sign-in. See [Sign in](user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).

**My set-password link doesn't work.**
Links last 72 hours and work once. A re-sent link replaces the old one. Ask your Overseas Admin to re-send it. See [Set your first password](user-manual/account-access/auth-002-first-password.md).

**How do I sign out on my phone?**
Menu (☰) → **My profile** → **Logout** in the website header. See [Sign out](user-manual/account-access/auth-006-sign-out.md).

## Leads
**Why can't I move a lead to Follow-up?**
Every open stage needs a next action with a due date first. See [Next action](user-manual/leads/lead-005-next-action.md).

**Why are some stages missing from Change stage?**
Counselors choose Qualified, Follow-up, Counselling Scheduled, Counselling Completed, or close the lead as Not Interested, Not Eligible or Lost. Other stages are set by telecallers or the system. See [Change stage](user-manual/leads/lead-006-change-stage.md).

**How do I reopen a closed lead?**
You can't; ask the telecaller manager or your Overseas Admin.

**When can I convert a lead to a student?**
From **Qualified** up to **Counselling Completed**, when no student is linked. See [Convert](user-manual/conversion/conv-001-convert-to-student.md).

**Does logging a "Not Interested" call close the lead?**
No. A counselor's call never changes the stage. Close it with [Change stage](user-manual/leads/lead-006-change-stage.md).

**Can I book a counselling appointment for my lead?**
No. Telecallers book them; you confirm them and record the outcome. See [Lead appointments](user-manual/calendar/cal-001-lead-appointments.md).

## Students and applications
**Where do I change an application?**
On **Students** (or **Applications**) → **Evaluate Applications**. The 360 Applications tab only lists applications. See [Advance stage](user-manual/applications/app-004-advance-stage.md).

**Why can't I mark an application Ready for submission?**
Every required checklist document must be **Verified** (or **Approved**, for a CV or SOP) or marked **Not required**. See [Sub-status and the gate](user-manual/applications/app-005-sub-status-ready-gate.md).

**How do I mark an application Enrolled?**
With **Enrolment** → **Confirm enrolment** and the confirmation document. **Advance stage** never offers Enrolled. See [Confirm enrolment](user-manual/arrival/arr-001-confirm-enrolment.md).

**Can I change an application's intake?**
Only when the university defers the student: **Record outcome** → **Deferred** → **New intake** replaces it. There is no other intake edit on the counselor screens.

**Who can reopen a closed application or correct a visa decision?**
Only the Overseas Admin.

## Documents and money
**Why isn't a document in Document Verification?**
Only documents tied to one of your applications are listed. Upload documents with their application chosen. See [Upload a document](user-manual/documents/doc-004-upload-document.md).

**Can I record a fee?**
No; the Overseas Admin records fees. You can upload payment proof and deposit receipts. See [Finance tab](user-manual/finance/fin-002-finance-tab.md).

## Reminders and tasks
**Where do reminder notices show?**
Reminder **tasks** appear on **Tasks**. Notices are emailed to you (and sent by WhatsApp/SMS if you opted in); counselors have no notifications page, so they don't appear inside the portal. See [Reminder rules](admin-manual/config/cfg-002-reminder-rules.md).

**Where did my cancelled task go?**
Cancelled tasks aren't listed. Mark tasks done instead if you may need them later. See [My tasks](user-manual/tasks/task-001-my-tasks.md).
