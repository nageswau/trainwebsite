# Quick-start guide: Career Counselor

> Verified 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` (School CRM documentation, sessions S2–S10).

## Role Purpose
The Career Counselor is EduSphere staff who record career guidance sessions, counselling notes and recommendations, set
students' career goals and preferences, run Soft Skills and Digital Skills batches, and manage funding-support cases,
for the schools in their portfolio.

## Login
1. Open the welcome email from EduSphere and set your first password (valid for 72 hours). See
   [Set your first password](../user-manual/account-access/auth-003-set-your-first-password.md).
2. Afterwards, sign in at **/overseas/login**. See [Sign in](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).

## Daily Workflows
1. Record each guidance or counselling session on the dashboard; move statuses as sessions happen.
2. Check records with **Follow-up Required** and their next follow-up dates.
3. Take attendance in your skills batch sessions; record assessment scores.
4. Move funding cases to their next stage.

## Restrictions
- You see only students of schools in your portfolio.
- Status changes follow a fixed order (Not Started → Scheduled → Completed → Follow-up Required).
- A closed funding case cannot be reopened.
- Individual counselling and Digital skills need Silver or higher.

## Common Problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This record was changed by someone else…" | Click **Discard my changes and reload** and edit again. |
| Career goal or batch status not updated after saving | Reload the page. |
| A school is missing from the batch **School** list | It has no students yet. |
