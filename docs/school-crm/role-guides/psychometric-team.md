# Quick-start guide: Psychometric Team

> Verified 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` (School CRM documentation, sessions S2–S10).

## Role Purpose
The Psychometric Team is EduSphere staff who assign psychometric assessments, attach the reports and record the
findings, for the schools in their portfolio.

## Login
1. Open the welcome email from EduSphere and set your first password (valid for 72 hours). See
   [Set your first password](../user-manual/account-access/auth-003-set-your-first-password.md).
2. Afterwards, sign in at **/overseas/login**. See [Sign in](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).

## Dashboard
**Assessments**, **Assign an assessment**, bulk entry and a **Student 360° view** list, all on one page.

## Daily Workflows
1. Assign assessments to new students.
2. When a report is ready, attach its address; the assessment becomes completed and the parents are told.
3. Record strengths, interests and recommendations.

## Restrictions
- You see only students of schools in your portfolio.
- A report address cannot be changed after attaching.
- Psychometric tests are part of every tier from Bronze; a school without an active tier is refused.

## Common Problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No students in your portfolio yet." | Ask your Overseas Admin. |
| "Each item must be 80 characters or fewer." | Shorten the item or split it with commas. |
| Session ended while typing results | Sign in again in a new tab, then click **Save results**. |
