# Quick-start guide: Academic Team

> Verified 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` (School CRM documentation, sessions S2–S10).

## Role Purpose
The Academic Team is EduSphere staff who record academic results (Draft → Verified → Published), IELTS/SAT preparation
and foreign-language classes, and keep Digital Portfolios, for the schools in their portfolio.

## Login
1. Open the welcome email from EduSphere and set your first password (valid for 72 hours). See
   [Set your first password](../user-manual/account-access/auth-003-set-your-first-password.md).
2. Afterwards, sign in at **/overseas/login**. See [Sign in](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).

## Dashboard
All your work is on one page: portfolio progress, results, test preparation, language classes, bulk uploads and the
student list.

## Daily Workflows
1. Enter new results as Drafts (single or CSV).
2. Verify Drafts uploaded by colleagues, then publish verified results (parents see them only when published).
3. Record test-prep scores and language certifications.

## Restrictions
- You cannot verify or publish a result you uploaded yourself.
- Drafts cannot be edited from the screen; check numbers before saving (marks above the maximum are accepted).
- You see only students of schools in your portfolio.
- Services outside a school's tier are refused (for example IELTS coaching needs Gold).

## Common Problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No students in your portfolio yet." | Ask your Overseas Admin to assign schools. |
| No **Verify**/**Publish** button | Another member must do it. |
| A wrong result was saved | Contact EduSphere support. |
