# Quick-start guide: Parent

> Verified 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` (School CRM documentation, sessions S2–S10).

## Role Purpose
A Parent follows their children's progress with EduSphere: career guidance, assessments, published results, activities,
skills, funding support and school moves. The Parent portal is read-only.

## Login
1. Open the invitation email from your child's school and click the link (valid for 7 days). Set your password. See
   [Accept a school invitation](../user-manual/account-access/auth-002-accept-an-invitation.md).
2. Afterwards, sign in at **/overseas/login**. See [Sign in](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).
3. If a second school links you to another child, you use the same login; no new invitation is sent.

## Dashboard
**My children** shows a card per child with status tiles. Below are **Upcoming sessions** and your latest
**Important notifications**.

## Daily Workflows
1. Open the dashboard and look at the status tiles.
2. Read new notifications.
3. Before a session, check **Upcoming sessions** for the date and time (IST).

## Restrictions
- You see only the children the school has linked to you.
- You cannot change anything; ask the school to correct details.
- Results appear only after the school publishes them.

## Common Problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No child linked to your account yet." | Contact the School Coordinator. |
| The unread count never goes down | Known behaviour: opening a notice does not mark it read. |
| An "Upcoming session" message shows an early-morning time | That time is UTC; use **Upcoming sessions** (IST). |
| Attendance tile says "Not marked yet" though your child attended activities | The tile is daily class attendance; activities are listed separately. |
