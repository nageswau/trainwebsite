# Quick-start guide: Teacher

> Verified 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` (School CRM documentation, sessions S2–S10).

## Role Purpose
A Teacher follows the students assigned to them, takes daily class attendance and keeps their students' Digital
Portfolios up to date.

## Login
1. Open the invitation email from your School Coordinator and click the link (valid for 7 days). Set your password.
   See [Accept a school invitation](../user-manual/account-access/auth-002-accept-an-invitation.md).
2. Afterwards, sign in at **/overseas/login**. See [Sign in](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-sign-in.md).

## Dashboard
**Your students** lists the students assigned to you, each with a **View** button, and a results summary below.

## Daily Workflows
1. Open **Attendance**, choose today's date, mark each student and save.
2. When a student achieves something, add it to their Digital Portfolio.

## Restrictions
- You see only students whose **Assigned Teacher** is you.
- You cannot mark attendance for a future date, or before a student was enrolled.
- You cannot add or edit students, reports or activities.

## Common Problems
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No students assigned to you yet." | Ask your School Coordinator to assign your students. |
| "This student is not assigned to you" | Same; the Coordinator sets the Assigned Teacher. |
| "This school's partnership expired on {date}." | Tell your School Coordinator. |
