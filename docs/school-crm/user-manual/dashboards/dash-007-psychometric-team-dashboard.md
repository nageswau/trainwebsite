# Psychometric Team dashboard

> Doc ID: DOC-SCH-DASH-007 · Verified on 2026-10-06 against `main` @ `ce1f07c2` · Role: Psychometric Team

## Purpose
Your single workspace: every Psychometric Team task is done on this page.

## Who Can Use This Feature
**Psychometric Team** members, for the schools in their portfolio.

## Prerequisites
The Overseas Admin has assigned schools to your portfolio.

## How to Access
The dashboard opens after you sign in. It is the only sidebar item.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Know the sections
| Section | What you do there | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Assessments | All assessments, newest first; **Attach report**, **Record results** / **Edit results** | [Attach a report](../psychometric-team/psy-002-attach-a-report.md), [Record results](../psychometric-team/psy-003-record-results.md) |
| Assign an assessment | Assign a test to a student | [Assign an assessment](../psychometric-team/psy-001-assign-an-assessment.md) |
| Bulk entry — assessments (CSV) | Upload many assessments | [Bulk assessments](../psychometric-team/psy-004-bulk-assessments.md) |
| Student 360° view | Open a student's 360° view | [Student 360° view](../student-360/s360-001-student-360-view.md) |

## Common Errors
**Problem:** "No assessments assigned yet." *(From code.)*
**Cause:** Nothing has been assigned in your portfolio.
**Resolution:** Assign an assessment.

## Tips
- Statuses show as `assigned` or `completed`. An assessment becomes completed when its report is attached.
