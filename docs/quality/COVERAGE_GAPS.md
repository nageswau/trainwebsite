## 7. What this gap list intentionally does not do

Per `CLAUDE.md`'s no-assumption rule, this list does not invent test assertions for genuinely open
business questions (exact GDPR retention periods, notification retry policy, batch-naming
convention, employer-approval workflow, commission trigger's exact "joined" semantics beyond
`DATA_MODEL.md` §6.3's technical resolution). A test cannot assert a rule that doesn't exist yet —
those remain open items in `PRD_OPEN_ITEMS.md`, not test gaps to close prematurely.
