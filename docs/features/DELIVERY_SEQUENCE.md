## Cross-wave note on parallelization

Waves 1–3 are **not** strictly sequential across the whole team — a team with separate
domestic/Overseas workstreams could run Wave 1 domestic-core and Wave 2 Overseas-core partially in
parallel once `FND-002`/`AUTH-001` land, since the two domains share almost no feature-level
dependencies (see `FEATURE_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md`'s four illustrative chains — domestic and Overseas
chains never cross except at the shared `FND`/`AUTH` root). Employer (Wave 3) is fully independent
of both once `FND-002` exists. This is relevant to BRD Risk R-01: parallelizing domains is the one
lever available to reduce elapsed time against the confirmed date, though it does not make the
4-day window credible on its own — see `docs/product/BRD.md` Risk R-01.

**Addendum, 2026-09-14** — School (Wave 4) is likewise fully independent of Waves 1–3 once `FND-002`
exists (it shares no feature-level dependency with the domestic, Overseas, Employer, or Agent
chains — see each `SCH-00x`'s `deps` field in `feature_catalog.json`), so a team with a dedicated
School workstream could run it in parallel with any of Waves 1–3, the same pattern as Employer.
