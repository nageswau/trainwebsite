# Quick-start guide: Agency Master

> Verified 2026-10-05 against docs commit `717d6aa8` (`main` @ `6a9be770`).

## Role Purpose
The Agency Master owns and runs the agency's EduSphere workspace: manages the team, sees every student and
application, confirms enrollments, reviews documents and claims commissions. An agency can have up to 3 active Masters.

## Login
1. Go to the Overseas sign-in page (`/overseas/login`).
2. Enter your email and password and click **Sign in securely**.
3. New agency? Register first — see [Register an agency](../user-manual/account-access/auth-001-register-an-agency.md).
   You can work once EduSphere has approved the agency.

## Dashboard
You land on the **Agent Dashboard** ("Whole agency · Your code {code}") with students, pipeline, documents,
commission, breakdowns by country and university, and a staff table.

## Daily Workflows
1. Open **Notifications** — deadline reminders and overdue tasks arrive each morning (08:00 India time).
2. Check **Documents > Pending** and review what staff uploaded.
3. Assign new students to staff (**Students > Assign**).
4. Look at **Tasks & Follow-ups > Overdue**.
5. Weekly: **Staff Performance** and **Reports**; claim **eligible** commissions.

## Restrictions
- Only Masters can: manage the team and staff permissions, archive or assign students, add/edit/delete agency
  universities, confirm enrollment, reject documents or request changes, see commissions, Staff Performance and the
  Staff performance / Commission reports.
- Applications only move forward; a withdrawn application cannot be changed.
- A deposit is read-only once paid.
