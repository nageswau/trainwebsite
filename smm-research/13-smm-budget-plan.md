and judge on cost per *qualified* lead and qualitative lead quality, not on statistical significance.

## 3. KPIs (measured, not estimated)

| KPI | Definition | Source | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thumb-stop rate (video) | 3-s plays ÷ impressions | Ads Manager | Hook quality |
| Hold rate | ThruPlays ÷ 3-s plays | Ads Manager | Story quality |
| CTR (link/form open) | clicks or form opens ÷ impressions | Ads Manager | Offer clarity |
| Form completion rate | leads ÷ form opens | Ads Manager | Form friction |
| CPL | spend ÷ leads | Ads Manager | Cost |
| **Cost per qualified lead (CPQL)** | spend ÷ CRM-qualified leads | Ads Manager + CRM | **Primary** |
| Counselling booking rate | bookings ÷ qualified | CRM | Sales follow-up |
| Attendance rate | attended ÷ booked | CRM | Lead quality |
| Organic: saves/shares per post | where visible in Insights (own account only) | IG/FB Insights | Content value |
| Comment-question volume by theme | count of tagged questions | manual log | Content backlog |

## 4. Weekly rules (draft, for human approval; `budget-pacer` doctrine)

- **Day 1–7:** no edits except fixing broken tracking or policy rejections.
- **Day 8:** pause any ad with < 50% of the median CTR **and** zero qualified leads after ≥ 2× the target CPQL spend.
- **Day 15:** move 20% of budget from the worst to the best ad set by CPQL (never > 20% per step).
- **Day 22:** refresh creatives where frequency > 3.5 and CTR has fallen > 30% from week 1 (`fatigue-detector` two-condition rule).
- **Day 30:** keep/kill per angle; document learnings; decide whether CMP-4 (placement-or-50%-back) is unlocked.

## 5. Preconditions before any spend

1. Meta Pixel + Conversions API verified (`pixel-conversions` skill); lead form → CRM sync tested; consent text approved (DPDP Act 2023).
2. Qualification definition agreed with the counselling team.
3. Contract gate status recorded (C18–C20 stay off until passed).
4. Ads built **paused**; a human publishes.
