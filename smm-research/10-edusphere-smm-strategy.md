# 10 — EduSphere Facebook & Instagram Campaign Strategy: Study in Dubai

Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: **DRAFT for review**. Nothing published, no ads created.

## 1. Strategic position

**The market sells promises; EduSphere should sell proof.**
Research (`06`, `07`) shows that the placement/fee-back promise is **not exclusive**. BIGS (Brookstone) advertises it directly, and at least four
consultants resell versions of it with inconsistent wording (AED 50k vs 55k, "guaranteed" vs "assured" vs "assurance email"). Nobody publishes the terms.
Nobody shows outcome denominators. Parent-trust and real-student content are rare (22 and 20 of 322 Dubai ads).

**Value proposition (draft):**
> "Study in Dubai with your eyes open. EduSphere shows you the real total cost, the awarding body, and the written terms of every
> placement promise before you pay, and supports you from counselling to arrival."

Proof points EduSphere must be able to show before using this line: a written cost sheet per programme, the partner agreements,
the counselling process, and (when available) cohort outcomes.

## 2. Positioning evaluation

Scoring 1–5 (5 = best). "Risk" is inverted (5 = lowest risk). Weights: Differentiation 25%, Credibility 25%, Compliance risk 30%, Appeal 20%.

| # | Message | Differentiation | Credibility | Compliance risk | Appeal (qual.) | Weighted | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Study in Dubai with career support | 2 | 4 | 5 | 3 | **3.6** | Publish now as the **base layer**; differentiate through specificity (C02) |
| 2 | Dubai education and employment pathways | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | **3.5** | Use with programme-specific internships only; avoid implying work rights |
| 3 | Conditional placement guarantee | 2 | 2 (until terms seen) | 1 | 5 | **2.3** | **HOLD**. Same claim as 4+ competitors; ASCI/CCPA exposure; Meta employment re-categorisation risk |
| 4 | Conditional 50% fee-back | 3 | 2 → 4 once terms are published | 2 → 3 with terms | 5 | **2.9 → 3.7** | **Run only after the contract gate**, framed as "Placement-or-50%-tuition-back (conditions apply)" with a public terms page. The transparency itself is the differentiator |
| 5 | Dubai student success and career development | 4 | 2 now → 5 with real cohorts | 4 | 4 | **3.4 → 4.3** | Build the evidence base now (consented stories); scale when the first cohort completes |

**Recommended sequence:** 1 + "transparency" (C16/C17) now → 2 and 5 as proof accumulates → 4 when the contract gate passes → 3 never as a standalone headline.

## 3. Facebook strategy

| Area | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Organic role | Trust and parent channel: FAQs, cost explainers, live sessions, community replies. FB reaches parents (P6) better than IG (hypothesis; validate with page insights) |
| Educational posts | Weekly "Dubai Study Facts" series: regulator check, cost sheet, intake calendar, visa process overview (sourced) |
| Student testimonials | Only consented, real students; show the course, the timeline and the struggle, not just the outcome; latest completed cohort only |
| Video | 1 FB Live per fortnight (parent Q&A in English; Malayalam session monthly); clips cut into Reels |
| Parent messaging | Safety, total cost, money flow, documents; avoid fear tactics |
| Enquiry generation | Instant forms with qualifying questions (`lead-gen-builder` method): graduation status, intake, budget band, programme interest, who decides (student/parent). Messenger/WhatsApp CTAs for Kerala |
| Paid concepts | `12` C01–C17 (C18–C20 held) |

## 4. Instagram strategy

| Area | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Reels (primary) | 3/week: myth-busters (C05, C16), day-in-the-life (C09), counsellor answers to DM questions; 20–40 s, burned-in subtitles, hook in the first 2 s on screen |
| Carousels | 2/week: cost breakdown, "5 clauses to read", programme comparisons, intake calendar |
| Short educational video | "60-second explainers": KHDA vs CAA, degree vs diploma, Emirates ID |
| Student-life content | UGC from enrolled students (paid creator agreement + consent): metro, campus, budget, weekends |
| Stories | Daily: polls ("Which intake?"), question sticker AMAs, countdowns to webinars, link sticker to the eligibility form |
| Highlights | Start Here · Costs · Programmes · Parents · Placement Terms (when approved) · Student Stories · FAQs · Contact |
| Influencer collaboration | Micro-creators (10k–100k) who are **current Dubai students from India** and Kerala education creators; disclose paid partnership (ASCI influencer guidelines) and brief them on banned claims |
| Community engagement | Reply to every comment within 4 working hours; route detailed questions to DM → WhatsApp; weekly "your questions answered" Reel built from real comments |
| Comment-to-DM | "Comment COST for the full cost sheet". Use only if replies can be staffed (manual or an approved automation tool; no new tool chosen here) |

Use one variable per test (`media-buyer` doctrine: one ad per angle, one broad ad set). Build everything **paused** for human approval.

## 6. Compliance rules for every post and ad

1. No "guarantee", "assured", "100%" or absolute words without a verified contract and legal sign-off. Never "100%" for abstract claims.
2. Every outcome number shows the period and the denominator (ASCI).
3. Testimonials only with written consent (CCPA coaching guidance; also good practice).
4. Never state work rights, visa timelines or Golden Visa eligibility without official sources.
5. State the study ≠ job disclaimer (D2) on any career-led ad.
6. Co-brand institution logos only with written partner permission.
7. The Malayalam copy in `12` (C11) must be checked by a native speaker before use.
8. Approval flow: Draft → Fact check (evidence link) → Compliance review → Partner review (if co-branded) → Approved → Scheduled.
