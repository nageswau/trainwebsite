## 2. What competitors repeatedly publish

1. **Event and fair promotion** (Fateh Admissions Days, StudyIn fairs, DMU/BITS at Najah 2026): the dominant enquiry CTA.
2. **Country/cost comparisons and visa-speed hooks** (Shiksha, Skymark "UK visa in 1 day", Leverage costs/part-time work).
3. **Testimonial reels**, mostly UK/Ireland/Germany journeys; few are Dubai-specific.
4. **Campus life and graduation** (Dubai branch campuses).
5. **Comment-to-DM** lead capture (upGrad "Comment PLAN", Leap).
6. **Vernacular content** for Kerala (Skymark: all observed reels in Malayalam).

## 5. Recommendations (feed into `10`, `11`)

- Use Facebook for **parents**: fortnightly Live Q&A (English + monthly Malayalam), cost and money-flow explainers, safety walkthroughs.
- Answer the unanswered questions above as a recurring series, with sources in every post.
- Mirror Skymark's vernacular reach in Kerala, but without the visa-speed or "100% job guarantee" style claims.
- Respond to comments publicly (when appropriate) to build visible accountability, the opposite of the refund complaints observed.
