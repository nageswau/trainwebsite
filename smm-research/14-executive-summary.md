## 1. The five things that matter

1. **The offer is not exclusive. EduSphere's own partner already advertises it.** BIGS = **Brookstone Institute of Global Studies, Dubai**
   runs "**Not Placed? Half Your Fee Comes Back**" to Indian audiences (Ad Library `2146143089665140`, active 2026-10-27). At least four consultants
   resell the same programme as "Guaranteed placement from AED 55,000*" (Confluence `1534089164699650`), "Minimum Assured Package AED 50,000"
   (Weconnect `1978014122873386`), or "100% Placement Assurance" (Best Career Counselling Surat `1869964704193821`). Leading with "Job Guarantee"
   makes EduSphere one more voice saying the same thing.
2. **No seller publishes the terms, and that is EduSphere's opening.** Brookstone's ad says "the conditions are published, not buried", but its
   website has no terms or placement page; its "Career Outcome" link points to `#`. The claims are inconsistent across sellers (AED 50k vs 55k;
   "guaranteed" vs "assured"; MBA vs a KHDA training institute that is "not degree-awarding"). **Recommended position: "Study in Dubai with your eyes open":
   written total cost, awarding body, and plain-English terms of any placement promise before payment.**
3. **The guarantee/refund ads must stay on hold** (concepts C18–C20) until EduSphere has *read* the signed contract (you reported it is signed and
   legal-approved, but the document has not been inspected: `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`). Legal sign-off must also cover **Indian** advertising rules (ASCI education
   guidelines: no unsubstantiated job guarantees, no "100%" abstract claims, mandatory disclaimer; CCPA 2024 coaching guidance), and Meta's
   employment-category risk. EduSphere's own backlog proposes banning "job guarantee" on its pages (seo-011 / DEC-SCOPE-234, proposed, not recorded).
4. **The market default is scholarship + intake urgency + "free counselling" + Apply Now → Meta lead form.** 322 Dubai-destination ads, about 100 advertisers.
   Under-served: **parents** (22 ads; rare organically), **real student outcomes** (20 ads), **total living cost** (no organic account shows it),
   and **Dubai-vs-other-countries comparisons** (none, although consultancies' comparison Reels are their best performers).
5. **Kerala is the vernacular battleground.** 76 Malayalam UAE-education ads (Skymark, TIIB, Dream Abroad…), several with aggressive "100% Job
   Guarantee / fee refund in 9 months" claims (for the UK: the closest public scheme returns 50% cash + 50% course credit, not a 100% refund).
   Students also show refund distrust in comments ("Please provide the refund of our registration fee asap!!!!", upGrad; "stop making promises you cannot deliver on", Leap).
