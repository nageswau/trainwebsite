## A. Career-support positioning (🟢/🟡)

### C01 — "The question to ask before you pay any Dubai institute" (P3, P1)
- **Hook (0–3 s):** "Every Dubai programme says 'placement'. Ask this one question first."
- **Headline:** What does 'placement' actually mean?
- **Primary text:** Before you pay a single dirham, ask: What counts as a job? By when? What must I do? Who pays if it doesn't happen? EduSphere counsellors walk you through the written terms of every Dubai programme we recommend, line by line. Free 20-minute session.
- **CTA:** Learn More → instant form (5 qualifying questions)
- **Format:** Reel 9:16, 30 s, talking head + on-screen checklist; FB feed 4:5 cut
- **Reel script:** 0–3 s counsellor to camera: "Every Dubai programme says placement…" · 3–12 s four questions appear as text cards · 12–22 s "We show you the contract clause for each one" (B-roll of a highlighted document, blurred) · 22–30 s CTA "Book a free terms walkthrough".
- **Visual prompt:** "Vertical video still, Indian female education counsellor, 30s, smart-casual, bright modern office, holding a printed document with highlighted lines, soft daylight, shallow depth of field, clean sans-serif text overlay area top third, no logos"
- **Required proof:** none beyond counselling-service description 🟢 · **Disclaimer:** D1
- **Conversion:** form lead (Qualified = graduate + budget band + intake)
