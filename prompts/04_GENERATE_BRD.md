# 04 — Generate Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Prerequisite:
High-impact Product Decision rows must be explicitly confirmed, blocked, or consciously deferred.

Read:
- CLAUDE.md
- PRODUCT_DECISION_REGISTER.md
- evidence/conflict documents

NO CODE.

Create `docs/product/BRD.md` containing:
- document control/version
- business background/problem
- business objectives
- success measures
- stakeholders
- confirmed user groups/roles
- business scope
- confirmed current release
- confirmed future scope
- exclusions
- business processes/journeys
- business rules
- information/document needs
- reporting needs
- integration needs at business level
- privacy/compliance obligations
- operational/support expectations
- assumptions explicitly approved
- dependencies
- risks
- open decisions
- change-control rules
- business acceptance criteria

Use stable Business Requirement IDs:
`BR-<DOMAIN>-###`

Anything unresolved goes to an annex and must not silently enter current scope.

Create:
- `docs/product/BRD.md`
- `docs/product/BRD_OPEN_ITEMS.md`
- `docs/product/BRD_CHANGE_LOG.md`

STOP for BRD approval.
