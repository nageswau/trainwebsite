**The key difference**

Your employees need **department dashboards**.

Your partners need **business dashboards**.

So don't show the partners 50 separate screens first. The Partner Dashboard should summarize the entire company and allow them to **drill down**:

**Business → Department → Employee → Student/Application/Transaction**

For example:

**Abroad Education Revenue ₹XX**  
→ Country  
→ University  
→ Agent  
→ Staff

→ Payment

→ Company

→ Candidate
