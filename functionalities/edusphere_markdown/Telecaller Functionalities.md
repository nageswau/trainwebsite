For **Edusphere**, the Telecaller CRM should focus on **lead generation → calling → qualification → follow-up → appointment → handover to Counselor/BDM → conversion tracking**.

**8. Lead Priority**

Use three levels:

**🔥 Hot**

Ready to join / immediate requirement.

**🟡 Warm**

Interested but needs follow-up.

**🔵 Cold**

Long-term / low interest.

Telecaller should be able to change priority based on the conversation.

**22. Telecaller Role-Based Access**

Telecaller should **not** have access to everything in the CRM.

**Telecaller can:**

✅ View assigned leads  
✅ Call leads  
✅ Add call notes  
✅ Schedule follow-ups  
✅ Send WhatsApp  
✅ Send email  
✅ Fix appointments  
✅ Assign to counselor  
✅ Update lead status  
✅ View own performance

**Telecaller should not:**

❌ Edit financial records  
❌ Modify application documents  
❌ Change university application status  
❌ Change counselor records  
❌ Delete leads  
❌ View confidential management reports  
❌ Modify employee targets

↓

Documents → Application → Offer → Deposit  
↓

Visa → Decision → Final Admission  
↓
